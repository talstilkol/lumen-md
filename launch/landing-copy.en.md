## Headline

**A note editor built for Hebrew**

## Subhead

Lumen is a local, open-source Markdown editor: your notes stay with you,
Hebrew behaves as it should, and every note is a plain file you can take
anywhere.

## Three pillars

- **Hebrew first.** A Hebrew interface, direction per line, search that
  understands prefixes and niqqud.
- **Local and private.** Notes stay on your device. No account, and it
  works offline.
- **Your files.** Every note is a Markdown file. Export everything in one
  click.

## Who it's for

- Students summarising lectures in Hebrew, with formulas and English terms.
- Researchers keeping sources, citations and field notes.
- Writers who want a quiet place to write, without a cloud they didn't
  choose.
- Developers who write documentation in Hebrew and English in one document.

## Calls to action

- Primary: **Open Lumen in the browser** (nothing to install, no sign-up).
- Secondary: **Join the beta** (GitHub Discussions).

## What isn't perfect yet (always on the page, never hidden)

It's a beta. There's no sync service of our own yet, co-editing is young,
there are fewer plugins than in Obsidian, and the phone apps aren't in the
stores yet. The full comparison, with sources: `docs/project/compare`.

## FAQ

- **What does it cost?** Nothing. It's open source, under the MIT licence.
- **Where are my notes?** On your device: in the browser's storage, or in a
  folder you picked. Not with us.
- **Can I move from Obsidian or Notion?** Yes: import an Obsidian vault or a
  Notion export with one command.
- **What about AI?** Optional. You can run a model on the device, and nothing
  goes to a cloud unless you choose it.
