Lumen is a Markdown editor for notes, research and writing, built for Hebrew and right-to-left from the start, and it works in English just as well.

HEBREW FIRST
• The interface in Hebrew, mirrored; also Arabic and six more languages.
• Direction is set per line, so an English term or a number inside a Hebrew sentence stays in place.
• Search finds words with a prefix and ignores niqqud.

YOUR NOTES, ON YOUR DEVICE
• No account needed. Notes are stored on the device, and nothing is sent anywhere by default.
• Works without a network.
• Every note is a plain Markdown file. Export the whole vault as a .zip or a folder, any time.
• Import an Obsidian vault or a Notion export.

WRITE THE WAY YOU THINK
• Source, split, preview and rich-text modes.
• Links between notes, tags, a graph of your notes, daily notes.
• Tables, math, diagrams and code blocks.
• Optional AI that can run on the device; nothing goes to a cloud service unless you set one up with your own key.

OPEN SOURCE
Lumen is free and open source under the MIT licence. Questions and feedback: GitHub Discussions.
