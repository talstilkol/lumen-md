Support and costs

Lumen is open source (MIT) and mostly built by one person. Every feature stays free and local. Money comes in only to pay for hosting, and only if you choose it.

This page runs no script and loads nothing from another site. Payment links open only when you click them.

Paid encrypted sync relay (optional, planned)

If you don't want to run your own server, you will be able to pay for a managed relay. Like the free relay you can run yourself, it sees only encrypted boxes. Encryption, the key and recovery stay on your device.
