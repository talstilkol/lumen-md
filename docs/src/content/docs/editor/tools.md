Each of these has a **?** button that opens this help at its own section.

## Theme settings

**Theme settings…** in the command palette changes the colours, the
accent, the editor's font, size and line height, and the width of the
text, live. The **Contrast** section shows whether the text and the
accent are readable against the background. **Export** saves the theme as a
file and **Import** reads one back.

## Voice memos and dictation

Speak instead of typing. The quick mode writes what you say straight into
the note with the browser's speech recognition. The memo mode records the
audio, turns it into text and a summary, and inserts them as a voice-memo
block. Under the local-only AI policy this runs on your device; nothing is
sent anywhere. A pill at the bottom shows that the microphone is on; its
stop button ends the recording.

## Canvas

A whiteboard beside your notes: sticky notes, text, shapes, arrows,
frames and images on an infinite canvas, with pan, zoom, undo and redo.
Each canvas is saved in the vault as a `.tldr` file and saved again as you
draw.

## Sync conflicts

When the same note changed on this device and in the cloud since the
last sync, Lumen shows both versions side by side. Changes that don't
overlap are merged for you; for each part that does, choose this device's
or the cloud's version. You can also keep one side as a whole, or cancel
and decide later.

## Dialogs

Questions and confirmations open as small dialogs. **Enter** confirms,
**Escape** cancels, and focus goes back to where it was.
