---
title: "Tutorial: your first Hebrew note"
description: Write a Hebrew note with a heading and lists, find your way in it with the outline, and save it — in ten minutes.
type: tutorial
---

In this tutorial you write a short Hebrew note: a heading, a bulleted list,
a list numbered in Hebrew letters, and a task. On the way you meet the smart
lists and the outline. You need Lumen open (on the web or the desktop app)
and nothing else.

1. **Start a note.** Press `⌘N` (`Ctrl+N` on Windows and Linux). Switch the
   view to *split* with `⌘2`: the Markdown on one side, the page on the other.
2. **Write a heading.** Type `# תוכנית לשבוע` and press `Enter`. The line
   turns right to left by itself, because it starts with Hebrew.
3. **Start a list.** Type `- לקנות חלב` and press `Enter`. A new bullet is
   already there. Type `- לקבוע תור` and press `Enter` once more.
4. **End the list.** On the empty bullet, press `Enter` again: the bullet
   goes away and you are out of the list.
5. **Number in Hebrew letters.** Type `## משימות`, `Enter`, then `א. לכתוב
   סיכום` and `Enter`. Lumen writes `ב.` for you. Type `לשלוח למורה`, press
   `Enter`, and see `ג.` appear.
6. **Make a sub-item.** On the empty `ג.` line, type `לבדוק שגיאות` and press
   `Tab`. The item moves in under `ב.` and becomes `א.` of a new sub-list.
   `Shift+Tab` brings it back.
7. **Turn it into a task.** With the cursor on any item, press `⌘Enter`. A
   box `[ ]` appears; press `⌘Enter` again to check it.
8. **Find a heading.** Open the outline (`⌘K` → *Outline*). Type `משימות` in
   its filter box: only that heading is left. Press `↓` and `Enter` to jump
   to it. The filter ignores niqqud, so `מְשִׂימוֹת` finds it too.
9. **Save.** Press `⌘S`. In a vault the note is saved there; otherwise Lumen
   asks where to save it.
