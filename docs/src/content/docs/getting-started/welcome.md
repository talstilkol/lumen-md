## Where Lumen fits

There's no shortage of markdown editors — Obsidian, Notion, Typora, Bear,
Logseq, Roam, HackMD, Joplin, Craft. Lumen sits at a specific intersection:

| | Lumen | Obsidian | Notion | Typora | Bear |
| --- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Local-first (works offline) | ✅ | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Web app (no install) | ✅ | ❌ | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Auto data → table + chart | ✅ | ⚠️ plugins | ⚠️ DB only | ❌ | ❌ |
| RTL / Hebrew first-class | ✅ | ⚠️ | ⚠️ | ⚠️ | ⚠️ |
| Mermaid + Graphviz + PlantUML + ABC + 3D | ✅ | ⚠️ plugins | ⚠️ Mermaid only | ⚠️ | ❌ |
| Real-time collab | ✅ WebRTC | ❌ | ✅ Pro | ❌ | ❌ |
| Smart search (embeddings) | ✅ Pro | ⚠️ plugin | ✅ AI | ❌ | ❌ |
| MCP for AI agents | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Local LLM (WebGPU) | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |
| Open source (MIT) | ✅ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ | ❌ |

## What we believe

**1. Plain text wins.** Lumen never invents a proprietary format — every
note is portable markdown. Rendering is opinionated; storage isn't.

**2. Data is content.** A spreadsheet inside a note shouldn't need a
plugin. Drop a CSV, JSON, or SQL block and it just becomes a table with
chart suggestions.

**3. AI assists, never gates.** Bring your own key. Disable AI entirely
and Lumen still ships every other feature.

**4. Privacy is structural, not promised.** Local-first storage, optional
end-to-end encryption for collab, opt-in telemetry that's off by default.
